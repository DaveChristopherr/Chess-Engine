About davechristopherrr

What is this?
This is a custom-built, highly optimized chess engine. It uses a Minimax algorithm with Alpha-Beta pruning, enhanced Move Ordering (Stockfish-lite logic), and Piece-Square Tables.

Who is davechristopherrr?
Created by Dave out of pure boredom, this bot is a digital reflection of the quiet pursuit of meaning. When life gets overwhelming, the 64 squares offer a sanctuary of logic and silence.
